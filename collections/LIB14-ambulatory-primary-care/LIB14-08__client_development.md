## Core content
### Partnership brief
- Problem (synthetic panel gaps)  
- Pharmacist scope under §157.101 / protocol [1]  
- What we will **not** do (diagnose; promise payment)  
- Privacy  
- Pilot metrics from quality toolkit  
- Exit criteria  

### Pilot evaluation (90 days)
Access days, completion, unresolved aging, clinician satisfaction (survey) — **not** HbA1c claims unless separately studied with IRB/QI designation.

## Setting branches
Skip any branch that does not apply to your operation. Synthetic examples only — no real patient or facility identifiers.

### Community (independent / retail)
Community pharmacists may enter Texas collaborative arrangements under §157.101 when statutory conditions are met [1]. Separate dispensing PIC duties from CPA drug-therapy management documentation.

### ASC / consulting pharmacy
ASC medication consulting is not ambulatory chronic-care CPA practice — keep LIB11 separate.

### Clinic-embedded
Primary setting. Clarify employment vs contract; incident-to supervision conditions are payer-specific and **held** here pending MAC/billing review [3]. ASHP ambulatory minimum standard orients practice infrastructure [2].

### Specialty / infusion / compounding
Specialty referral from primary care may need LIB15 access workflows; do not promise specialty network acceptance.

### Telepharmacy / delivery
Virtual visits still require scope, privacy, and payer rules verification. Do not invent telehealth billing codes.

## Local adaptation — Texas verification checklist
- [ ] Confirm Texas Occ. Code §157.101 applicability to the practice setting (community vs FQHC/hospital/academic conditions in statute) [1].
- [ ] Verify protocol/order content meets board minimums; medical diagnosis not delegated [1].
- [ ] Confirm protocol filing/copy requirements that apply to the chosen setting [1].
- [ ] Supervision adequacy elements documented (physician relationship, periodic review) [1].
- [ ] BAA/employment/HIPAA posture for PHI [5].
- [ ] Billing: **no claim of eligibility** until billing specialist + counsel review CMS BPM / MAC rules [3] (C14-003 held).
- [ ] TSBP pharmacist license active; any additional registrations verified.
