## Core content — workflow
1. **Contract / agreement** — PIC vs consultant scope; cadence; accessibility; BAA [1][7].  
2. **Onboarding review** — baseline facility checklist (template pack).  
3. **Periodic visit** — meet 7-day rule if applicable [1]; tour pharmacy/ADC/floor stock.  
4. **Findings** — separate observations vs required CAPAs; cite rule when claiming shall.  
5. **Facility ownership** — each CAPA has facility owner + due date (consultant does not “own” nursing practice unilaterally).  
6. **Close** — effectiveness check; report to P&T/quality.  
7. **Board-ready file** — agreement, visit notes, perpetual C-II, diversion pathway, EP annex [1].  
8. **Escalations** — theft/significant loss → DEA clock [6]; PIC change → 10-day notice [2].

### Evidence discipline
Texas Class C May 2024 PDF is the controlling state text for consultant definition, written agreements, on-site cadence, PIC limits, perpetual C-II, and EP P&P topics [1]. CMS §416.48 and EP Rule apply when the ASC participates in Medicare [3][4]. ASHP/AORN sources are practice frameworks for perioperative medication safety and diversion programs [8][9][10]. U10-01 is closed: freestanding ASC ≤100 beds follows the seven-day on-site rule under current May24 text.

## Setting branches
Skip any branch that does not apply. Synthetic examples only — no real patient or facility identifiers.

### Community (independent / retail)
Community Class A sites generally are not Class C institutional models — do not apply 7-day on-site or PIC multi-facility bed limits blindly. If a community pharmacy also contracts to consult an ASC, keep licenses and agreements separate.

### ASC / consulting pharmacy
Primary setting. Facility definition includes ASC licensed under H&S Ch. 243; ≤100 beds requires pharmacist on-site at least every seven days and continuous accessibility [1]. Medicare ASCs add 42 CFR §416.48 pharmaceutical services and CMS EP alignment [3][4]. Written consultant/PIC agreement must be Board-available [1].

### Clinic-embedded
Hospital Class C sites with ≥101 beds require continuous on-site pharmacist supervision when open for pharmacy services (rule exceptions apply — read primary text) [1]. PIC multi-site limits (≤3 facilities or 150 beds for ≤100-bed facilities) constrain consulting portfolios [1].

### Specialty / infusion / compounding
Perioperative high-alert medications and CS floor stock raise diversion and safe-use stakes. Use ASHP perioperative and diversion guidelines plus AORN ASC medication-safety literature as practice discussion — not Texas substitutes [8][9][10].

### Telepharmacy / delivery
Remote chart or ADC review can support a consulting model but does **not** replace required on-site cadence under Texas Class C [1]. Document the 24/7 accessibility method in the written agreement.


## Local adaptation — Texas + CMS verification checklist
- [ ] Confirm facility includes ASC licensed under H&S Ch. 243 in Class C definitions [1].
- [ ] ≤100 beds: pharmacist services part-time/consulting as needed; pharmacist **on-site at least once every seven days**; accessible to respond at all times [1].
- [ ] ≥101 beds: continuous on-site pharmacist supervision when open for pharmacy services (with limited exceptions in rule) [1].
- [ ] Consultant may be PIC; **written agreement** shall exist; copy available to Board [1].
- [ ] PIC ≤100 beds: PIC for no more than **three facilities or 150 beds**; written agreement if part-time/consulting [1].
- [ ] PIC responsibilities: diversion controls, formulary, distribution, emergency drugs, records, legal operation, ADC oversight when used [1].
- [ ] P&P include emergency preparedness plan; C-II perpetual inventory; C-II waste witnessed/cosigned [1].
- [ ] Incoming PIC notifies TSBP within **10 days** [2].
- [ ] Medicare ASC: 42 CFR §416.48 pharmaceutical services [3]; CMS EP program alignment [4].
- [ ] DEA effective controls + Form 106 clock [5][6].
- [ ] PHI consulting → BAA [7].
