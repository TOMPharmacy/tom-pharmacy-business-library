## What this helps you decide or do

Monitor assumptions, cash, and unfinished launch work in the first operating quarter **without arbitrary industry targets**. Metrics are dictionaries you populate; literature on closures and payment pressure [9][19][20] explains *why* monitoring matters, not what your target must be.

## Setting branches

### Community (independent / retail)
Apply open-door vs closed-door, hours, counseling space, and delivery footprint. Keep sterile compounding out of Class A-only workflows unless the sterile subclass path is confirmed with the board. Use local need evidence; do not invent script volume or ROI.

### ASC / consulting pharmacy
Separate facility medication-use accountability from consultant pharmacist scope. Confirm institutional / Class C sequencing and HHSC (or equivalent) facility status before promising start dates. Document who owns procurement, storage, and controlled-substance records in writing.

### Clinic-embedded
Clarify limited formulary vs full community dispensing. Watch entity boundaries (clinic vs pharmacy FEIN) for billing and ownership. In Texas, Class D shall not be issued to a physician's office — verify class fit before filing.

### Specialty / infusion / compounding
Branch early on 503A vs 503B, sterile classification, prior-authorization load, and COGS float. Do not paste USP chapter text; link when verified. Keep all patient and dollar examples synthetic and labeled fictional.

### Telepharmacy / delivery
Separate remote verification roles from driver logistics. Multi-state practice needs location-specific registrations. Allocate delivery cost as its own cash line so logistics margin stays visible on the dashboard.

## Additional operating notes

### Evidence discipline
Every material “shall” in launch conversations must trace to statute, regulation, or board/agency page with a retrieval date [see Sources]. Secondary blogs are discovery only. Journal articles on turnover and access [9][18][19] support diligence framing, not numeric guarantees.

### Coordination with later collections
- Ownership change after open → LIB02.  
- Daily request-to-handoff → LIB03.  
- Payer cash and denials → LIB05.  
- Workforce/PIC coverage → LIB06.

### Synthetic multi-setting stress questions
1. If we add delivery next quarter, which counseling/delivery rows reopen?  
2. If an ASC asks us to manage their med room, are we selling retail Class A services or Class C consulting?  
3. If sterile demand appears, do we stop and re-class rather than “just start”? [1][10]

Document answers in the decision log before changing marketing copy.


## Deepening the draft (final-pass notes)

### Decision hygiene
Record the date, setting label, and evidence links whenever you change class hypothesis, add delivery, or open sterile scope. Texas applications cancel if ownership, managing officer, or location changes after review begins — keep the deal calendar and the Board calendar reconciled.

### What “good enough to proceed” looks like
- Need statement with at least two independent evidence sources (interviews, public hours maps, CHNA excerpts — link only).
- Class hypothesis matched to services with sterile explicitly in or out.
- Cash CSV critical rows filled from quotes or explicitly deferred with a stop condition.
- Named owners for DEA, NPI, inspection, and wholesaler letter.
- Held claims listed on the opportunity brief, not hidden in footnotes.

### Failure patterns to avoid
- Marketing a launch date before Stage 0–4 gates pass.
- Assuming NCPDP credentials appear automatically with NPI.
- Treating SBA break-even formulas as pharmacy ROI benchmarks.
- Copying licensed association curricula or USP chapter text into this pack.
- Using disaster-era telephonic counseling flex language as if it were permanent default (see LIB03).

### Reviewer prompt (for ChatGPT batch + human)
Summarize: purpose, risk tier, Texas checks, held claims, and any must/shall without a numbered cite. Flag invented numbers. Human PIC/counsel still required for L2/L3.


### Cross-checks before external use
1. Every numeric field is user-entered or labeled FICTIONAL.
2. Every must/shall has a numbered source.
3. Held table is visible near the end — not deleted for “cleanliness.”
4. Setting branches that do not apply are marked N/A rather than deleted (preserves audit trail).
5. File remains under `/workspace` only until Owner authorizes publish channels separately.


## Second expansion block

Re-read setting branches before go-live of any new service line. Re-run the Texas checklist when PIC, hours, delivery, compounding, ownership, or payer mix changes. Keep claim-ledger status=held items on huddle agendas until resolved or explicitly accepted as out-of-scope with owner initials.

Partner links: stock exceptions → LIB08; payment exceptions → LIB05; workforce coverage → LIB06; downtime → LIB09; privacy/BAAs → LIB07. Do not invent state fee-schedule numbers, DIR calculators, or technician ratios while those rows remain held.
