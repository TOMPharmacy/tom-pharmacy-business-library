### ASC / consulting pharmacy
Primary B2B motion: ASC administrator + written Class C consultant agreement [4]. Visit cadence and PIC limits live in LIB11; this pack supplies ethical outreach and proposal exclusions.

### Clinic-embedded
Medical director and clinic administrator are the buyers. Immunization service-line discovery may reference VFC only after the CDC provider URL is manually re-proofed (**unverified** this pass) [13].

### Specialty / infusion / compounding
Sell to health-system or specialty clinic leadership as organizational purchasers. Keep outcomes claims bounded and BA-gated when PHI is involved [5].

### Telepharmacy / delivery
Advertise pharmacy services in Texas only when licensed as required; do not induce Texas residents to use unlicensed out-of-state pharmacies [1][2]. Ch.102 patient solicitation how-to remains **HELD** [3].
